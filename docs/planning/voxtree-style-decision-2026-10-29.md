# VoxTree — Video Style Decision
**Decided:** Oct 29, 2026 by Goding (voice call, confirmed "Yes")
**Status:** Locked default for the $500 / Nov 28, 2026 pilot launch

## The default

- **Visual:** Stylized Pixar-like 3D clone of the parent/grandparent. A storybook version of them — recognizable likeness, warm/exaggerated animation, not photorealistic.
- **Voice:** Their real, authentic cloned voice. Do NOT stylize, cartoon, or pitch-shift the voice. The authenticity is in the audio.
- **Framing language:** "A storybook version of you" — never "a digital you" / photoreal clone.

## What this is NOT

- Not a photorealistic face/body clone as the default (research: deepfake concern, lower comfort for older adults, uncanny risk).
- Not the real parent sitting in a chair for the full story video as the default product — that was discussed as the Ms. Rachel counterpoint, not adopted as the default.
- Not a generic cartoon character unrelated to the person — likeness must still read as that specific parent/grandparent.

## Why (research basis)

- Oct 29 research, 36 sources, moderate confidence — no direct head-to-head study of family clones exists.
- Kids 2-6 attend more to animated human characters; stylized products are what scaled (Wonderbly, Vooks, Gramms voice-only); photoreal sells mainly to corporate training.
- YouGov: 63% very concerned about deepfakes — stylization lowers that barrier for grandparents buying/gifting.

## Pipeline implications for pilot test videos

1. Character generation: optimize for stylized likeness from parent/grandparent photos — identity capture -> Pixar-style character, not photoreal avatar models (e.g. HeyGen/Hedra photoreal paths are fallback/comparison only).
2. Voice: Chatterbox clone, trained/checked for authentic timbre — voice QA is the pass/fail gate, not visual photorealism.
3. Test-video acceptance check:
   - Does a family member instantly recognize the character as that person?
   - Does the voice sound like them at normal listening, no cartoon effect?
   - Does a 2-6 year old stay with the animated character through the story?
4. Optional hybrid (not locked): real photo / short real video of the grandparent as intro/outro bookends around the stylized story. Use only if it doesn't add render cost/complexity to the pilot.

## Open / not decided by this

- Exact stylization level for ages 5-7 (research suggests slightly less exaggerated for older kids).
